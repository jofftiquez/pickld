# Moderator playbook

Draft for founder review. Last updated 13 November 2026. For moderators only; the public rules are the Community Guidelines below.

## Before you start

- Photos are blurred. Read the source, reason, caption and scores first, then press Show photo only if you need to see it to decide.
- Take a break whenever you need one. Step away after anything distressing and tell a founder.
- Never decide about your own content or about people you know personally. Skip the item and leave it for someone else.
- Never copy, screenshot, download or share anything from the console.

## Where items come from

- Auto-check: the automatic check scored the photo or comment as borderline. It stays hidden until you decide. The second opinion is a hint, not a verdict.
- Reports: a reader reported a jar, moment, comment or profile. It usually stays visible while it waits; a moment reported by three people within 24 hours is hidden until you decide.
- Appeals: the owner asked for a review of a removal. You can't decide an appeal on a removal you made.
- Escalated: a moderator wasn't sure. Any moderator can decide it.

## Deciding

- Keep (K): it doesn't break a guideline. The item closes and the content stays or goes live.
- Remove (R): it breaks a guideline. Pick the guideline first. The owner sees the reason and gets a strike.
- Remove and warn: the same as Remove, recorded as a warning. Use it when the owner should take extra note, for example a second removal for the same thing.
- Escalate (E): you aren't sure, the case is new, or it involves a possible crime. Add a note saying why.
- Jar and profile reports can only be kept or escalated. Escalate a profile that clearly breaks the guidelines and tell a founder.
- On an appeal, Keep overturns the removal and clears the strike; Remove upholds it. Judge it fresh against the guidelines, not against the first decision.
- Every decision goes into the audit log with your name. It can't be edited or deleted, so write notes you'd be happy to read back.

## Guideline by guideline

### Nudity or sexual content

- Remove nudity, sexual acts and clearly sexual poses. Swimwear at a beach or pool is fine.
- Breastfeeding, art in a museum and medical photos without sexual focus are fine.

### Harassment or bullying

- Remove photos or captions that mock, threaten or shame a real person, and anything that shares someone's address or phone number.
- Friendly teasing between people in the same photo is usually fine. When the target looks upset or unaware, remove.

### Violence or dangerous acts

- Remove gore, serious injuries, threats and dangerous challenges.
- News, sport and fiction (a film poster, a costume) are fine.

### Hate speech or symbols

- Remove attacks on protected groups and hate symbols. A symbol shown to criticise it, as in a museum or a history lesson, can stay; add a note.

### Spam or scam

- Remove ads, repeated posts, fake giveaways and impersonation.

### Involves someone under 13

- If the owner looks under 13, escalate and note why. A founder checks the account; under-13 accounts are deleted.
- Remove a photo of a child that exposes, sexualises or endangers them, and escalate.

### Something else

- Use it only when no other guideline fits, and always add a note.

## Child-safety material

- A match against known child sexual abuse material never reaches the queue. The system removes it, suspends the account and alerts the founders.
- If you see anything that might sexualise a minor, don't open it again and don't download it. Remove it under Nudity or sexual content, add the note “possible child safety”, and tell a founder straight away. The founders own the legal report.

## Strikes and suspension

- Every removal still standing counts as a strike, whether automatic or yours. Three in 90 days suspends the account for 7 days, and the suspension lifts by itself.
- An overturned appeal clears its strike and lifts the suspension if the owner drops under three.
- A child-safety suspension has no end date and only a founder can lift it.

## Targets

- Work oldest first. Aim to decide reports and appeals within a day (proposed target for the founders to confirm).
- An item waiting more than 2 hours raises an alert to the founders.
