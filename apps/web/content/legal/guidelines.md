# Community Guidelines

Draft for founder review. Last updated 13 November 2026.

Pickld is a photo diary. Each day you fill a jar with up to 8 moments from your own life, and the people who read you see your day. These guidelines say what belongs in a jar and what happens when something doesn't.

## The short version

- Share your own day. Post photos you took, of things you were part of.
- Be kind to the people in your photos and the people reading them.
- Keep Pickld for people 13 and over.
- If something feels wrong, report it. Reports are anonymous.

## What isn't allowed

Every photo and caption is checked before anyone else sees it. Our moderators also review what people report. We remove anything in these groups.

### Nudity or sexual content

- Nudity, sexual acts or sexually suggestive photos.
- Anything that sexualises a minor. We remove it and report it to the authorities.

### Harassment or bullying

- Photos or captions that mock, shame, threaten or target a person.
- Posting someone in a way meant to embarrass them, or after they asked you not to.
- Sharing someone else's private information, like their address or phone number.

### Violence or dangerous acts

- Graphic violence, gore or serious injury.
- Threats, or encouraging anyone to hurt themselves or others.
- Dangerous stunts or challenges that could get someone hurt.

### Hate speech or symbols

- Attacks on people for their race, ethnicity, nationality, religion, caste, sex, gender, sexual orientation, disability or illness.
- Symbols, slogans or imagery that promote hate groups.

### Spam or scam

- Ads, repeated posts, fake giveaways or links meant to sell or mislead.
- Pretending to be someone else.

### Involves someone under 13

- Pickld is for people 13 and over. Accounts of younger people are deleted.
- Photos of children are fine in a family moment, but never in a way that exposes, sexualises or endangers them.

### Something else

- Anything illegal where you or the people in the photo are.
- Content that breaks these guidelines in a way the groups above don't cover.

## What happens when something is removed

- The moment is hidden and you see why it was removed.
- Each removal counts as a strike. Three strikes within 90 days suspends your account for 7 days. While suspended, others can't see your jars and you can't post, comment or follow, but you can still see your own diary.
- A match against known child sexual abuse material suspends the account with no end date and is reported as the law requires.

## Asking for a review

- If you think we got it wrong, tap Request a review on the removed moment. You can ask once per removal.
- A different moderator from the one who removed it decides. If they agree with you, the moment comes back and the strike is cleared.
- You can still ask for a review while suspended.

## Reporting and blocking

- Report a jar, moment, comment or profile from its menu. The person won't know who reported them.
- Block anyone you don't want to see or hear from. Blocking removes follows both ways and neither side is told.

## Private moments

Moments you keep private are only for you. Other people can't see or report them, and they stay out of feeds, shared links and notifications.
